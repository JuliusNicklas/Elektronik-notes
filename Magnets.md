# Magnetic field
Depends on material permeability ($\mu_r$)

Three material types: (relating to magnetism)
1. Diamagnetic ($\mu_r < 1$) – Weakly magnetised in opposite direction -> $|\vec{B}_{int}| < |\vec{B}_{ext}|$
2. Paramagnetic ($\mu_r \approx 1$) – Almost no magnetisation -> $|\vec{B}_{int}| \approx |\vec{B}_{ext}|$ 
3. Ferromagnetic ($\mu_r >> 1$) – Strongly magnetised with the external field -> $|\vec{B}_{int}| > |\vec{B}_{ext}|$
